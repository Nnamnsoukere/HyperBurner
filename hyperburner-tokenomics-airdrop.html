How the airdrop works

When a milestone counts
Market cap means the HB price on the main HB/SOL pool multiplied by the circulating supply, which is total supply minus burned HB (3,400,000 HB). We read it from DexScreener. A milestone is confirmed only when the market cap stays above the target for 24 hours in a row, so a short spike does not trigger it.
Who qualifies
A wallet needs at least 2,000 HB, and its balance must have stayed at or above 2,000 HB for the 7 days before the snapshot. Each wallet is counted on its own, so splitting tokens across many wallets does not add up. Buying just before a snapshot does not qualify.
Who is excluded
The liquidity pool and its vaults, the burn address, and the project's airdrop and community wallets are excluded, along with any exchange-owned wallets. Without this, the pool itself would count as the largest holder and take most of every reward. The full excluded list is published before the first snapshot.
How rewards are split
Each qualifying wallet gets a share of the milestone pool in proportion to its HB balance, and no single wallet can receive more than 2% of a milestone pool (5,000 HB at $1M, 3,000 HB at $3M, 4,000 HB at $5M). The leftover from capped wallets is shared among the other qualifying wallets.
Example. Suppose all qualifying wallets together hold 400,000 HB at the $1M snapshot. A wallet holding 4,000 HB owns 1% of that total, so it receives 1% of 250,000 HB, which is 2,500 HB. The numbers are only an illustration.
Where the tokens wait
The 1,100,000 HB sit in a multisig wallet whose address is public from day one. After each distribution we publish the snapshot data and the transaction links so anyone can check the result.
Changes to the rules
These rules are final once published. Any change is announced publicly before the next snapshot is scheduled, and never changes a snapshot that has already been taken.

Airdrops depend on the market-cap milestones being reached and are not guaranteed. HB is a community token, not an investment, and its price can fall to zero.
