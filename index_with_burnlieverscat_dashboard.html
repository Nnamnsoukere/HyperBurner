AIRDROP RULES • PUBLISHED BEFORE LAUNCH

Fair rules, written first.

Every rule below is public before a single HB is distributed, so nobody has to guess how a snapshot works.

WHEN A MILESTONE COUNTS24 hours above target

Market cap is the HB price on the main HB/SOL pool multiplied by circulating supply (5,000,000 minus burned HB, so 3,400,000 HB). We read it from DexScreener. A milestone is confirmed only after it stays above the target for 24 hours in a row.

WHO QUALIFIES2,000 HB for 7 days

A wallet must hold at least 2,000 HB, and keep that balance for the 7 days before the snapshot. Wallets count one by one, so splitting tokens across many wallets does not add up.

WHO IS EXCLUDEDPool, burn, project

The liquidity pool and its vaults, the burn address, and the project's airdrop and community wallets, plus any exchange-owned wallets. The full list is published before the first snapshot.

HOW REWARDS SPLITProportional, 2% cap

Each qualifying wallet gets a share in proportion to its HB balance. No wallet can receive more than 2% of a milestone pool (5,000 / 3,000 / 4,000 HB). Anything left from capped wallets is shared among the others.

WHERE THE TOKENS WAITPublic multisig wallet

The 1,100,000 HB airdrop pool is held in a multisig wallet with a public address. Snapshot data and distribution transactions are published after each payout.

CAMPAIGN POOL500,000 HB, no marketing wallet

Campaign rewards are paid only from the airdrop pool. Category amounts are announced before each campaign opens, the total can never exceed 500,000 HB, and no new HB is ever created.

WORKED EXAMPLE

How one share is calculated

The numbers below are only an illustration of the method.

01 Qualifying wallets together hold 400,000 HB02 One wallet holds 4,000 HB, which is 1% of that03 At the $1M snapshot it receives 1% of 250,000 HB04 Result: 2,500 HB, under the 5,000 HB cap

Airdrops depend on the market-cap milestones being reached and are not guaranteed. HB is a community token, not an investment, and its price can fall to zero. These rules are final once published. Any change is announced publicly before the next snapshot is scheduled, and never alters a snapshot that has already been taken.
